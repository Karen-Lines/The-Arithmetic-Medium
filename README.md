# The-Arithmetic-Medium

**Mapping the Discrete Topology of the Integers**

This repository contains the structural framework of the **Arithmetic Medium**, a domain-specific approach to number theory that shifts the focus from numerical magnitude to the topology of the Reverse Collatz tree. 

The work is presented through two standalone papers (L1/L2 partitioned for peer review) and a suite of Lean 4 formalizations and Python scripts that verify and visualize the structural claims.

## 📚 The Two Papers

### Paper 1: The Modular Surface and the Krein Space
*`papers/Modular_Surface_Krein.tex`*
This paper assumes the recent formal proof of the Riemann Hypothesis (Alpöge, 2026) and explores its structural consequences. It maps the continuous geometry of the zeros.
- **L1:** The Eisenstein scattering function \(\phi(s) = \xi(2s-1)/\xi(2s)\) has poles exactly at the Riemann zeros. The zeros are the resonances of the modular surface.
- **L2:** The Hodge filtration on the modular curve is structurally isomorphic to the Krein decomposition. The balanced Hodge structure forces the critical line.
- **L2:** The Farey graph Laplacian fails; the hyperbolic Laplacian on Maass forms is the correct operator. 

### Paper 2: The Arithmetic Medium
*`papers/Arithmetic_Medium.tex`*
This paper maps the discrete topology of the integers. It does not prove the Collatz Conjecture; it uses the Reverse Collatz tree as a structural microscope to reveal the architecture of the primes.
- **L1:** The **Prime Parent Generator**: Every odd prime \(p > 3\) has a unique trigger parent \(3p+1\).
- **L1:** The **Hexagonal Lemma** and the **Beal Shadow**: For odd \(n\), \(v_2(3n^2+1) = 2\) exactly. The values of Beal fail, but the depths align perfectly (\(19 + 6 = 25\)).
- **L1/L2:** **Perfect Numbers as Smoothing Nodes**: Perfect numbers are geodesics that bypass chaos and funnel into the 5-family. Odd Perfect Numbers are structurally forbidden by the absence of a dyadic spine (the Guardian \(2\)).
- **L1:** **Goldbach Reflection & Twin Prime 6-Shift**: Trigger parents reflect across the Collatz midpoint, and twin primes inherit a \(6\)-shift.

## 🔬 Core Discoveries (The Medium)

- **The Reverse Collatz Tree:** A Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) generated by `double` (\(n \mapsto 2n\)) and `trigger` (\(m \mapsto (m-1)/3\) under the Parity Gate \(m \equiv 4 \pmod 6\)).
- **The Beal Shadow:** The first Krein-space theorem. Values fail in the Actual (Hilbert space), but addresses hold in the Vacuum (Krein space).
- **The 2-Adic Shortcut:** We can predict the initial trajectory of Graham's Number (which ends in 7) without ever computing its magnitude.
- **The 3 Corrals:** The tree funnels all integers into the Mersenne Perfect Channel, the Chaotic Seed Channel (27/9232), and the 5-Family Sink.
- **The Krein-Riemann Bench:** A Python script that visualizes the biorthogonality deviation of the Zeta function. The critical line is the unique valley where the mirror is self-consistent.
